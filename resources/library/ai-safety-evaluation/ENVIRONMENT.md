# AI Safety and Evaluation Environment and Metadata

- **Practice type:** evaluation design, annotation quality, safety metrics, failure analysis, and launch decisions.
- **Resources:** the public synthetic [`ai-quality-safety`](../../data/ai-quality-safety/) SQLite database and CSV tables, plus the SQL and Python topic folders when analysis is needed.
- **Data grain:** inspect `schema.sql` before querying. Keep joins within this dataset; do not introduce fields from another schema.
- **Coverage:** quality rubrics, human agreement, harmful-output taxonomies, thresholding, offline and online evaluation, red-teaming, monitoring, and safe rollout.

Personal attempts, feedback, scores, company context, and coverage status belong only in `private-progress/`.
