# Statistics and Experimentation Question Catalog

- Identify sampling bias and propose a representative design.
- Interpret a confidence interval correctly.
- Distinguish statistical from practical significance.
- Explain Type I, Type II, and power trade-offs for a decision.
- Design an A/B test with unit, guardrails, and attrition plan.
- Diagnose multiple-comparison risk and segment heterogeneity.

## Applied inference

- Define population, sample, parameter, and statistic for a product question.
- Distinguish random variation from selection bias.
- Choose between mean, median, and trimmed mean for a skewed metric.
- Interpret correlation without making a causal claim.
- Explain confounding and propose a design to reduce it.
- Calculate and interpret a relative lift and an absolute lift.
- Estimate sample size drivers for a two-arm experiment.
- Explain sequential testing risk and a safe decision process.
- Handle noncompliance and attrition in an experiment.
- Define a stopping rule before observing results.
- Review an experiment result with a primary metric and guardrail metrics.
- Communicate uncertainty to a non-technical stakeholder.
