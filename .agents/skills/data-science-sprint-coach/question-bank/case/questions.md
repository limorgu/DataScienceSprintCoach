# Case Question Catalog

- Structure an ambiguous business problem into a decision, evidence, and recommendation.
- State a target, data, assumptions, and critical missing information.
- Propose a baseline, metric, guardrails, and launch criterion.
- Diagnose a metric change while separating correlation from likely cause.
- Present a concise recommendation with risks and a next validation step.

## Data science case depth

- Frame a recommendation system problem and select success and guardrail metrics.
- Decide whether a metric change is a measurement issue, behavior change, or product change.
- Estimate impact with explicit assumptions and sensitivity analysis.
- Choose data sources and identify likely quality gaps.
- Propose features while checking leakage, fairness, and serving feasibility.
- Compare simple and complex model options against the decision need.
- Design a rollout with holdout, ramp, and rollback criteria.
- Diagnose a launch that improved the average but harmed a key segment.
- Recommend the next analysis when evidence is incomplete.
- Present an executive summary with decision, confidence, risks, and owner.
