# Progress Evidence Rules

The training leaf tracks evidence, not exposure.

## Score scale

| Score | Meaning | Progress treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Independent/excellent | PASS |
| 4 | Pass with minor refinement | PASS |
| 3 | Partial | REPEAT |
| 2 | Substantive help | REPEAT |
| 1 | Major gap/not answered | REPEAT |

## Mastery threshold

Do not call a skill mastered from one success.

A pattern may move to higher difficulty only after:
- two independent passes;
- at least one is a transfer/unseen variant;
- no immediate regression.

## Trend threshold

- one result = event;
- two comparable results = early signal;
- three or more comparable results = trend may be described.

## Session checkpoint

Every session must end with:
- question count;
- last question number;
- next question number;
- active schema;
- covered patterns;
- weak/retry patterns;
- progression instruction.

This checkpoint is required even if a detailed tracker also exists.
